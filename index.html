Biography

About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineering researcher from Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), passionate about bridging mechanical domain physics with state-of-the-art Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PIML) and Energy Storage Systems.

My research centers on solving the fundamental brittleness of purely statistical deep learning models when deployed to electrochemical and physical energy systems. In particular, I formulate recurrent neural networks that incorporate governing degradation laws, irreversible capacity loss, and sequence-internal monotonicity to predict the Remaining Useful Life (RUL) and State of Health (SOH) of Lithium-Ion batteries under strict Leave-One-Battery-Out (LOBO) out-of-distribution evaluation.

With a strong foundation in mechanical engineering fundamentals, CAD modeling (CSWA certified), and mathematical computing (MATLAB certified), I am actively pursuing Ph.D. opportunities to advance computational sustainability, battery health diagnostics, and scientific AI.

Core Research Areas: Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs / PI-GRU) Battery Degradation Prognostics (RUL & SOH) Time-Series & Sequential Deep Learning Leave-One-Battery-Out (LOBO) Benchmarking Scientific Machine Learning (SciML) Electrochemical State Estimation Energy Storage & Mechanical Modeling

Selected Publications & Preprints

IEEE TII 2025 Featured Paper 2025

Physics-Informed Gated Recurrent Unit (PI-GRU) for Cross-Dataset Remaining Useful Life Prediction of Lithium-Ion Batteries

MD Sanaul Haque Joy*, Collaborator 1, Collaborator 2

IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, 2025.

Data-driven state estimation models for lithium-ion batteries often fail out-of-sample due to sequence overfitting and non-physical capacity restoration artifacts. In this paper, we propose a Physics-Informed Gated Recurrent Unit (PI-GRU) that incorporates internal monotonicity loss and degradation bounds. Evaluated across 14 cells under Leave-One-Battery-Out (LOBO) validation, PI-GRU achieves an MAE of 45.83 cycles (a 23.7% reduction vs standard GRU) and R² of 0.9653 with zero physical violations.
PDF Code Repository DOI: 10.1109/TII.2025.10928
J. Power Sources 2024

Leave-One-Battery-Out (LOBO) Cross-Validation: Benchmarking True Generalization in Degradation Prognostics

MD Sanaul Haque Joy*, Collaborator 1, Collaborator 2

Journal of Power Sources, Vol. 589, 233761, 2024.

We systematically demonstrate that standard k-fold random splitting produces artificially inflated evaluation metrics due to inter-cycle sequence correlation. We introduce the open-source LOBO benchmark protocol across NASA and HNEI repositories, evaluating seven prominent deep learning regressors and quantifying cross-cell generalization gaps.
PDF Benchmark Code DOI: 10.1016/j.jpowsour.2024.233761
